## The moon's face: CGI Moon Kit (NASA)

The colour map of the moon's surface is from the CGI Moon Kit of NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, made from
the images of LROC, the wide-angle camera of the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. Public domain.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
